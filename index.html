A collection of parameterized physics simulations, mathematical visualizations, and rendering experiments.

scroll
Physics 01
Gravity Simulation

N-body gravitational attraction solved via Runge-Kutta 4th-order integration. Particles attract, collide elastically, and form emergent orbits.
Rendering 02
Raycaster

2D line-segment ray casting. Rays find the closest wall intersections to simulate light and shadow.
Algorithms 03
Quadtree

Spatial partitioning tree for efficient 2D proximity queries and collision detection.
Mathematics 04
Lissajous Table

Parametric curves from two perpendicular oscillations. Frequency ratios produce distinct closed forms.
Mathematics 05
Rotating Lissajous

Lissajous figure with a continuously evolving phase angle — the curve breathes and morphs over time.
Waves 06
Phaseshift

Visualizing how phase offset between two signals transforms their superposition.
Physics 07
Circular Motion

Uniform circular motion — angular velocity, centripetal acceleration, and phase projection onto axes.
Geometry 08
Rotating Polygons

Nested polygons inscribed inside each other at incremental rotation offsets.
Physics 10
Flocking

Reynolds' boids — separation, alignment, and cohesion produce emergent flocking. Spatial-hash neighbour queries keep it fast at scale.
Physics 11
Double Pendulum

Chaotic motion via RK4 integration — overlaid copies fan out from a hair's-breadth perturbation. Switch to a tuned pendulum-wave bank.
Creative 12
Flow Field

Thousands of particles drift along a Simplex noise field, leaving fading ribbons. The field slowly morphs, so the streamlines never settle.
Algorithms 13
Game of Life

Conway's cellular automaton — four rules on a paintable grid. Drop a Gosper glider gun, or seed noise and watch order emerge.
Waves 14
Wave Interference

A ripple tank — circular waves from multiple sources superpose into shifting interference fringes. Click to add or drag emitters.
Rendering 15
Reaction-Diffusion

The Gray-Scott model — two chemicals react and diffuse into corals, spots, and mazes. Paint to seed, and dial in feed / kill live.
Geometry 16
Voronoi / Delaunay

Drifting sites carve the plane into nearest-neighbour cells, with the dual Delaunay mesh overlaid. A natural companion to the Quadtree.
Creative 17
Slime Mould

Physarum agents sense a scent trail ahead and turn toward it, depositing as they go. The diffusing map grows emergent transport networks.
Physics 18
Cloth

A Verlet-integrated spring mesh you can grab, drag, and tear. Constraints relax into a draping fabric and snap when over-stretched.
Algorithms 19
Maze & Pathfinding

A recursive-backtracker maze, then watch A*, Dijkstra, BFS, or DFS flood the grid and backtrack the shortest route to the exit.
WebGPU 20
Gravity Simulation · GPU

The N-body sim reborn: tens of thousands of particles via Barnes-Hut (O(n log n)), rendered as a glowing 3D galaxy on the GPU. Orbit with the mouse.
Mathematics 21
Fourier Epicycles

Draw any shape and a chain of rotating circles re-traces it — a discrete Fourier transform made visible. Companion to Lissajous and Circular Motion.
Creative 22
Pong Wars

Two territories at war — every ball flips the enemy tile it hits to its own colour and ricochets, so the frontier sloshes back and forth forever. Built as a stream waiting screen: live scoreboard, overlay, and chat-driven hooks.
Physics 23
Fluid Simulation

A GPU Navier–Stokes fluid you can finger-paint — drag to inject dye and force, then drop in cylinders, airfoils, slits and funnels to watch the flow shed vortices around them. Bloom, sunrays, and a deep bench of solver sliders.
